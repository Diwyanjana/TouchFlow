Connected in three steps

  1. Open TouchFlow on your PC

    It shows a QR code and a 6-digit PIN.

  2. Scan the code with your phone camera

    Your phone's browser opens the touchpad and pairs on its own. Prefer typing? Enter the PIN instead.

  3. Start moving your finger

    The cursor follows. Tap to click, use two fingers to scroll.

Everything a remote needs

One app replaces the mouse, the keyboard shortcuts and the clicker you keep losing.

Touchpad with real gestures

Drag to move, tap to click, long-press or two-finger tap for right click, two-finger drag to scroll, tap-and-drag to move things.

Type on your PC with your phone

Use your phone's own keyboard, plus Esc, Tab, arrows, Ctrl, Alt, Shift, Win and one-tap shortcuts like copy and paste.

Media and volume

Play, pause, skip and change the volume from the couch.

Slide remote

Start the show, go forward and back, black out the screen and end it, all from your pocket.

Buttons you design yourself

Make a button that presses a shortcut, types a line of text, clicks, or controls media. Pick its name, icon and color.

Game pad

An on-screen joystick for WASD or arrow keys, plus hold-to-press buttons you can remap.

Stays on your Wi-Fi

Commands travel between your phone and your PC and nowhere else. Pairing uses a short-lived code, and only paired phones can control your PC.

Tray app that stays out of the way

Close the window and it keeps running in the tray. Optionally start it with Windows.

Send files both ways

Move photos and documents between your phone and your PC over your Wi-Fi. Nothing is uploaded to the internet.

Share text and paste it

Send text from your phone to your PC clipboard and paste it with one tap, or fetch what you copied on your PC.

Questions

Do I need to install anything on my phone?

No. The touchpad opens in your phone's normal browser. You only install TouchFlow on your PC.

Do I need internet?

Not to control your PC. Your phone and PC talk to each other over your home or office Wi-Fi, and both need to be on the same network. TouchFlow can optionally check once a day whether a newer version is available, and you can turn that off.

Windows asks about the firewall. What should I do?

Tick Private networks and choose Allow access. That lets your phone reach TouchFlow on your Wi-Fi. The installer sets this up for you, so you only see the prompt with the portable version.

Why does Windows say "Windows protected your PC"?

TouchFlow is new and doesn't have a code-signing certificate yet, so Windows shows this for any unfamiliar app. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Does it work in games?

Keyboard controls work in many games, so the joystick and buttons are great for single-player games and emulators. Moving the camera with the touchpad often doesn't work in shooters, because those games read the mouse differently. Games with anti-cheat may block software input, so try a single-player game first.

Can it control Task Manager or installers?

Windows ignores input from normal apps in admin windows. To control those, run TouchFlow as administrator.

What about Mac and Linux?

Not yet. TouchFlow is Windows-only for now.

Does vibration feedback work on my phone?

Haptic feedback works on Android. iPhone browsers don't allow it, so it stays silent there.
